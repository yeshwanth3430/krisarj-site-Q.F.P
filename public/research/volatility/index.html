Volatility Models

How big the moves are, and how that changes.

What this section covers

Measuring and forecasting volatility: the GARCH family for conditional variance, latent stochastic-volatility processes, realized measures built from intraday data, and the jump, regime and multivariate models layered on top.

Why it matters

Volatility is the input every risk number and option price depends on. Forecast it well and sizing, margin, VaR and option edge all follow.
