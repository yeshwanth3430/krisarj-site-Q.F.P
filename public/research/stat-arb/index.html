Statistical Arbitrage & Mean-Reversion Models

Trading the spread back to fair.

What this section covers

The stat-arb toolkit: stationarity and cointegration tests, mean-reverting processes and half-lives, pairs and relative-value construction, and the nonlinear, cost-aware and timing models that decide when to enter and exit.

Why it matters

Most systematic relative-value trading reduces to one question: is this spread mean-reverting, and is the reversion worth the costs? These models answer it.
