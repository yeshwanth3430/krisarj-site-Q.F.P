Financial Risk & Tail-Risk Models

How much you can lose, and how badly.

What this section covers

Quantifying loss: return distributions, VaR and expected shortfall, extreme value theory for the tails, conditional and simulation-based risk, decomposition and drawdown, stress testing, and liquidity, counterparty and model risk.

Why it matters

Risk management is the part of the job that keeps you trading. These models size the loss you can survive and the one that ends the account.
