High-Frequency & Microstructure Models

What happens inside the spread.

What this section covers

Tick-level modelling: point processes for order arrivals, duration and count models for trade timing, queueing models of the limit order book, price-discovery and market-impact models, and the market-making and optimal-execution frameworks built on them.

Why it matters

At high frequency the price is not a number but a queue. These models say how orders arrive, where you sit in the book, what your trade costs the market, and how a market maker quotes without getting run over.
