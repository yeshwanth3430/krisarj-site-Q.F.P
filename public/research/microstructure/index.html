High-Frequency & Market-Microstructure Models

What happens inside the spread.

What this section covers

Tick-level modelling: quote data and price discovery, point processes for order arrivals, duration and count models, queueing models of the limit order book, and the market-making and optimal-execution frameworks built on them.

Why it matters

At high frequency the price is not a number but a queue. These models say how orders arrive, where you sit in the book, and what your trade costs the market.
