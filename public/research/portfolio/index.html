Portfolio Optimization & Allocation

Turning views into position sizes.

What this section covers

Allocation from Markowitz mean–variance through Black–Litterman, risk parity and diversification objectives, growth and CVaR criteria, covariance estimation, real-world constraints and costs, and hierarchical, dynamic and attribution methods.

Why it matters

A good signal badly sized loses money. Optimization is where expected return, risk, costs and constraints meet a number of lots.
