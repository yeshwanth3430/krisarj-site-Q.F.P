Traditional Time-Series Models

How a series moves through time.

What this section covers

Modelling prices, returns, spreads and rates through time: the classical ARIMA family, multivariate and cointegration models, nonlinear and regime-switching models, state-space filtering, and spectral and decomposition methods.

Why it matters

Mean reversion, lead–lag relationships and regime detection are all time-series questions. This is the toolkit that turns a price history into a testable claim about tomorrow.
