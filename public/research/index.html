How this track is organised

Three catalogues, in the order a researcher meets them: Volatility Models (how big the moves are), Time-Series Models (how a series moves through time), and High-Frequency & Microstructure Models (what happens inside the spread). Each section opens its families one at a time.

Why this track matters

Research is what separates a hunch from a position. These are the models that turn raw market data into a measurable claim — about risk, about mean reversion, about where the next fill will come from — and the ones a desk expects you to name, fit and criticise.
