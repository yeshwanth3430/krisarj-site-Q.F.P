Optimal Control & Stochastic Optimization

Deciding, repeatedly, under uncertainty.

What this section covers

The control toolkit: convex and dynamic programming foundations, stochastic control and HJB equations, market-making and quoting problems, execution control, and risk-sensitive and numerical methods.

Why it matters

Market making, execution and dynamic hedging are control problems, not one-shot predictions: the right action now depends on inventory, impact and what you expect to do next.
