Options Trading & Risk Management

Pricing, Greeks and staying alive.

How this track is organised

First learn what an option is worth — Option Pricing Models. Then learn what to do with them — Option Strategies, forty of them from rookie to all-star. More sections will join as the track grows.

Why this track matters

Options are where the maths meets the market: every strategy is a portfolio of calls and puts, every P&L is a payoff diagram, and risk management is knowing — before entry — exactly where you make money, lose money and break even.
