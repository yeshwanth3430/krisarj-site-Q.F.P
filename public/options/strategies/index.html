How this subject will work

One strategy per lesson, in playing order — rookies first, all-stars last. As each lesson opens it gets the full treatment: the payoff at expiry drawn from zero, when to play it, the Greeks that drive it, and a Nifty-lots example with real breakevens.

Why this subject matters

Options are where the maths meets the market: every strategy is a portfolio of calls and puts, every P&L is a payoff diagram, and risk management is knowing — before entry — exactly where you make money, lose money and break even.
