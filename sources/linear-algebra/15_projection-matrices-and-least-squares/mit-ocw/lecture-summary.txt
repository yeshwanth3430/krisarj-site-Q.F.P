vector b onto the space spanned by the columns of A. If b is perpendicular to
the column space, then it’s in the left nullspace N ( A T ) of A and Pb = 0. If b is
in the column space then b = Ax for some x, and Pb = b.
A typical vector will have a component p in the column space and a compo­
nent e perpendicular to the column space (in the left nullspace); its projection
is just the component in the column space.
