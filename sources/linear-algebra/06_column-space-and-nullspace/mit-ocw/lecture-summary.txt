Column space and nullspace
In this lecture we continue to study subspaces, particularly the column space
and nullspace of a matrix.

Review of subspaces
A vector space is a collection of vectors which is closed under linear combina­
tions. In other words, for any two vectors v and w in the space and any two
real numbers c and d, the vector cv + dw is also in the vector space. A subspace
is a vector space contained inside a vector space.

Column space of A
The column space of a matrix A is the vector space made up of all linear combi­
nations of the columns of A.

Solving Ax = b

If there is a solution x to Ax = b, then b must be a linear combination of

    Big question: what b’s allow Ax = b to be solved?
    A useful approach is to choose x and ﬁnd the vector b = Ax correspond­

combination of columns of A.
    The system of linear equations Ax = b is solvable exactly when b is a vector
in the column space of A.
