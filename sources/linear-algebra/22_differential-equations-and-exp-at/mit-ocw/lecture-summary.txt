Stability
Not all systems have a steady state. The eigenvalues of A will tell us what sort
of solutions to expect:
1. Stability: u(t) → 0 when Re(λ) < 0.
2. Steady state: One eigenvalue is 0 and all other eigenvalues have negative
real part.
3. Blow up: if Re(λ) > 0 for any eigenvalue λ.
